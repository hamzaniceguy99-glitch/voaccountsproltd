Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do you do audits?

No. We're not registered auditors and we don't carry out statutory audit work. Most small companies are exempt, and if yours isn't we'll help you find a registered audit firm.

How are your fees charged?

Fixed, agreed in a letter of engagement before any work starts, with monthly payment available. Phone calls and emails during the year aren't billed separately.

Can you advise on pensions or investments?

No. That's regulated advice and we're not FCA authorised. We'll handle the accounting and tax and refer you to a regulated adviser.

I'm behind on my filings. Can you help?

Yes, and it's common. Tell us how far behind and we'll work through it in order, dealing with HMRC and Companies House as we go.

Which software do you use?

Whatever suits you, including the main cloud packages. If you're on spreadsheets and MTD applies, we'll explain what has to change.

Can you take over from my current accountant?

Yes. We'll write for professional clearance and handle the handover — it's a routine process.

Deadline approaching?

A free first meeting on where things stand and what it would cost. No obligation.
